ABOUT US

Nestled in the heart of Paisley, Renfrewshire, Paisley Sound Music Academy was founded in 2018 by local multi-instrumentalist Cole Kirkpatrick. What began as a humble single-room studio has grown into a vibrant creative hub dedicated to nurturing local talent.

Our tight-knit team of four professional tutors brings decades of industry and teaching experience. We offer tailored lessons in Music Theory, Instruments, Vocals, and Composition for all ages and skill levels. Whether you are picking up an instrument for the first time or prepping for high-level exams, we provide a warm, encouraging environment where music always comes first.
