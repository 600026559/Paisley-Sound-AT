Accessibility Statement

Home|Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: October 20, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Paisley Sound (the "Site," "we," "us,") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you visit, use our services from "www.paisleysound.fake" (the "Site").

We want as many people as possible to be able to use this website. For example, that means you should be able to:

  • Change colors, contrast levels, and fonts using browser settings.
  • Zoom in up to 200% without the text spilling off the screen.
  • Navigate most of the website using just a keyboard or speech recognition software.
  • Listen to most of the website using a screen reader (including the latest versions of NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver).

Compliance Status

This website is partially compliant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1 AA standard, due to the non-compliances and exemptions listed below.

Non-Accessible Content

We know some parts of this website are not fully accessible yet:

  • Some embedded audio, video, or media players may lack full keyboard navigation controls or alternative text descriptions.
  • Some older images or graphical elements may missing descriptive alternative text (`alt` tags).
  • Some color combinations may not meet minimum visual contrast ratios across all screen modes.

What We Are Doing to Improve Accessibility

We are actively working to improve the accessibility and usability of Paisley Sound. Our ongoing efforts include:

  • Ensuring logical visual and visual focus indicators for keyboard navigation.
  • Regularly checking site pages with automated audit tools and screen readers.
  • Adding proper ARIA labels and HTML5 landmark structures to audio content and player components.

Feedback and Contact Information

If you need information on this website in a different format, or if you find any accessibility problems not listed on this page, please get in touch with us:

Email: contact@paisleysound.com

We aim to respond to all accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

Enforcement Procedure

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 (the 'accessibility regulations') in the UK. If you are not happy with how we respond to your complaint, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
