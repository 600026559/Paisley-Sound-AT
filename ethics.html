Ethics and Safeguarding Statement

Home|Ethics and Safeguarding Statement

Last Updated:October 20, 2025

1. Safeguarding & Student Protection

The safety, mental health, and physical well-being of our students—especially children and vulnerable adults—is our primary responsibility.

  • Vetting & Background Checks: All tutors, instructors, and staff undergo comprehensive background checks (including Scotland’s PVG Scheme / UK Disclosure checks) prior to delivering lessons.
  • Transparent Teaching Spaces: In-person lessons take place in observable studio environments with clear sightlines, open-door policies, or glass panels to ensure visibility.
  • Professional Boundaries: Tutors maintain clear professional boundaries at all times, including across digital channels, social media, and online messaging.
  • Online Lesson Safety: Virtual tuition is conducted through secure, approved video platforms with clear guidelines for parental supervision for students under 18.

2. Inclusivity, Diversity & Respect

Music belongs to everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, or skill level.

  • Zero Tolerance for Discrimination: We strictly prohibit discrimination, harassment, or bullying based on age, race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, socio-economic status, or disability.
  • Adaptive Learning: We embrace diverse learning needs, providing supportive approaches tailored for neurodivergent students, physical adaptations, and unique cognitive styles.
  • Respecting Adult Learners: We recognize that adult students balance busy lives, performance anxiety, and distinct personal goals. We provide a respectful, judgment-free space that treats adult learners as full partners in their education.

3. Pedagogical Integrity & Healthy Technique

We believe encouragement and solid pedagogical principles produce the best musicians.

  • Positive Reinforcement: We reject pressure tactics or harsh criticism. Feedback is always constructive, empathetic, and designed to build musical confidence.
  • Physical Health & Ergonomics: Instructors prioritize healthy technique—including posture, breath control, and ergonomic hand alignment—to protect against physical strain or vocal fatigue.
  • Student-Centred Goals: Whether a child is preparing for graded examinations or an adult is playing purely for enjoyment, we tailor our instruction to match each student’s personal ambitions.

4. Professional Conduct & Intellectual Property

We run our music school with transparency and respect for creative communities.

  • Licensing & Copyright: We respect songwriters and publishers by utilizing properly licensed sheet music, audio tracks, and instructional materials.
  • Fair Pricing & Clear Policies: Our fee structures, booking terms, and cancellation policies are transparent, fair, and clearly communicated.
  • Continuous Professional Development: Our instructors commit to regular practice, pedagogical improvement, and keeping up to date with best teaching practices.

5. Privacy & Data Ethics

We respect the personal data and privacy of our students and families.

  • Data Protection: Student details, lesson records, and payment information are kept securely and handled in accordance with UK GDPR.
  • Media Consent: We never share or publish photographs or video recordings of students—especially children—without explicit, written consent from the student or their parent/guardian.
